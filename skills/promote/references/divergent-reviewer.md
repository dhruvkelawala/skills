# Divergent lens

Apply the shared review contract supplied by the parent to the selected transcript and pinned boundary. Look beneath the obvious correction for a blind spot or a counterexample that changes its proposed prevention.

Check whether a local fix leaves sibling callers broken, a successful test exercised only a lucky path, a claimed verification lacks an artifact, or a scope assumption caused unintended effects. Challenge a lesson that would punish a valid exception or enforce a mistaken review finding. Evidence of what was omitted must be observable in the reviewed history, not inferred from silence alone.

Search for an evidenced missed skill trigger or late invocation. Cite the triggering moment and catalogue/path evidence rather than recommending unrelated skills. Separate demonstrated corrections from hypotheses needing investigation; label the latter uncertain. Return candidates in the shared contract's format, including contrary evidence rather than manufacturing a contrarian finding.
