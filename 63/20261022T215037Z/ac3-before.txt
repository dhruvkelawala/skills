$ resume step 1 in skills/issue-to-pr/references/resume.md
5:1. Work on the recorded branch. When another worktree has it checked out (`git worktree list`), continue in that worktree. Otherwise run `git switch <branch>` here. Then `git status` must be clean and `HEAD` must equal the recorded head or the PR's `headRefOid`. When the branch cannot be selected this way, record `blocked: branch not selectable` and stop, before any repair.
